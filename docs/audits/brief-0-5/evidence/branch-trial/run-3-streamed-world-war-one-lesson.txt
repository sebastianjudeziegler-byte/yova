The opposing **Triple Entente** brought together:

These groupings reflected existing tensions. France feared Germany, Russia competed with Austria-Hungary for influence in the Balkans, and Britain worried about Germany’s growing military and naval power. Governments increased their armed forces and planned how they would fight alongside friendly powers.

This created a dangerous cycle: one bloc’s preparations were intended to increase its security, but the rival bloc saw them as a threat and prepared more intensely in response. Europe therefore became both divided and heavily armed.

A useful cause map is:

Instead, they created a system through which a local confrontation could expand. Alliances were the connections in the system; tensions and military preparations made those connections dangerous.
