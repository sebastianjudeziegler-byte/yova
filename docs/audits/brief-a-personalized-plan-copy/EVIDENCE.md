## What changed

- The fixed-slot provider receives `profile_summary` and `starting_context`, plus the method already chosen by deterministic code. Its validated per-session objective and profile-referencing method reason survive materialization. Session count, topics, mode, timing and order remain code-owned.
- Initial-plan method routing combines eligible task families with saved examples-first, hints-first, short-focus and reflective signals. Explicit choices and observed-evidence authority retain precedence.
- A named starting difficulty such as ETC becomes a planning priority after prerequisites and receives a larger duration budget within existing capacity limits. It is named in the rationale, without adding placement or learning evidence.
- Fallback objectives and checks use plain learner-facing copy; amount labels say “topic” and “practice check”. Whole-word title behavior is preserved from current main and shared with other bounded copy.
- Scheduling journeys use the existing preview-server clock header and a frozen browser Date. The existing explicit fixed-date journey remains intact; the elapsed-timer test advances its frozen clock deliberately. No runtime assertions were removed.
- The founder-only invited-tester environment change and reversal are prepared in [ROLLOUT.md](ROLLOUT.md). The percentage setting alone is not an invite filter.
